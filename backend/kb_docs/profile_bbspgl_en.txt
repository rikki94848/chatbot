BBSPGL PROFILE (Brief)
----------------------
BBSPGL (Balai Besar Survei dan Pemetaan Geologi Kelautan) is a technical implementing unit under the Geological Agency,
Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM), Republic of Indonesia.
Core mandate: to conduct surveys and mapping in marine geology to provide marine geoscience data, information, and thematic maps.

Outputs include:
- Marine geology data and maps (physical, geological, geochemical, marine geophysics).
- Thematic information for planning, marine resources management, marine geohazard mitigation.
- Public service: data services and survey/mapping services under applicable regulations.
