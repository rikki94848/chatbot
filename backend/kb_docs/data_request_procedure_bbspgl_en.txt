PROCEDURE FOR REQUESTING MARINE GEOLOGY DATA (General Operational Summary)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Note: Specific forms, SLA, license/fee terms follow official announcements on BBSPGL/ESDM portals.

Typical steps:
1) Preparation
   - Official request letter (institution/individual per policy).
   - Applicant identity and contact.
   - Data needs description (area/coordinates, parameters, time range, preferred format).
2) Submission
   - Submit via official channels listed on the BBSPGL portal (see links below) or service email.
3) Verification
   - Officers verify completeness, data availability, and intended use.
4) Approval & terms
   - Notification of approval/decline; if applicable, license terms and service fees per regulations.
5) Data delivery
   - Provided in the agreed format (e.g., shapefile, GeoTIFF/raster, CSV, documents) with metadata.
6) Technical support
   - Technical assistance may be provided as per policy.

Official channels:
- BBSPGL/BLU Portal: https://mgi.esdm.go.id/
- Contact us (BBSPGL): https://mgi.esdm.go.id/kontak-kami/
- Geological Agency BBSPGL profile: https://badangeologi.id/bbspgl
