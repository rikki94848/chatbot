BBSPGL DUTIES & FUNCTIONS (Outline)
-----------------------------------
General basis: organizational and governance provisions under the Geological Agency, Ministry of ESDM.

DUTY
- To carry out surveys and mapping in the field of marine geology.

FUNCTIONS (high-level)
1) Preparing plans, programs, and budgets for marine geology activities.
2) Conducting marine geology surveys (e.g., marine geophysics, geology, geochemistry).
3) Producing marine geology maps and thematic map products.
4) Managing, processing, and presenting marine geology data & information.
5) Delivering survey/mapping services and data/information services to users.
6) Managing facilities and infrastructure (survey vessels/labs) as applicable.
7) Handling legal affairs, cooperation, and administrative support.

Official references:
